I am working on a Kalshi BTC 15-minute binary prediction-market trading bot. The bot currently uses a fair-value probability engine for P(BTC settlement > strike) and then places small IOC limit orders when the model's probability edge is large enough. I need arXiv research that can help turn recent retrospective candidate filters into robust, forward-validatable strategy components.

Current local findings from recorded live v28 trades:

- Baseline live v28 replay: 632 entries, +$13.61 net, W/L 285/336 plus 11 flat.
- Brownian/FPT sanity gate: keep entries when v28 edge >= 3c, seconds_to_close >= 120, and 0.70 <= abs_d_sigma <= 1.10. Same-window replay +$27.37, but train-only dynamic walk-forward was mixed, so it may be a replay peak.
- Depth-decay/fillability gate: require depth/required >= 3, book_age <= 750ms, ask <= 80c, seconds_to_close >= 600. Same-window replay +$21.42 and parameter neighborhood was stable, but walk-forward margin was thin.
- Hybrid FPT + fillability gate: require edge >= 3c, depth/required >= 8, book_age <= 750ms, ask <= 83c to 85c, seconds_to_close >= 120, and abs_d_sigma <= 1.10 with lower bound around 0.7 to 0.85. Same-window replay around +$15.85 to +$26.13 depending on variant. This was the best dynamic train-only walk-forward family: 6/6 positive multi-schedule runs and 6/6 beat-live runs.
- Consensus/conformal proxy using v22-v28 probability disagreement improves hit rate, but low coverage and weak walk-forward make it more likely to be a risk supervisor than a standalone strategy.
