Search arXiv and related research for methods that convert calibrated probabilities into PnL-aware trade/no-trade thresholds for a small, nonstationary binary prediction-market strategy.

Project context:
- We trade Kalshi BTC 15m binary markets by estimating P(side settles true), comparing fair value to ask/fees, and gating execution with a robust liquidity/expiry filter.
- Current local replay snapshot has 470 matched filled v28 trades and 469 settlement labels.
- A capped-ACI probability overlay on Brownian terminal probability improves calibration and appears useful as a risk filter.
- Latest rolling walk-forward threshold test:
  - For each split, train/calibrate on 200 rows, pick the robust overlay threshold from train PnL only, then test the next slice.
  - Candidate aci_p_calibrated locked threshold 0.70 in all 4 splits.
  - Aggregate WFA result: 70 test entries, 38/31 W/L plus 1 flat, +$11.46, 16.4c avg/entry, 25.9% coverage, 4/4 positive test windows.
  - The stricter fixed overlay robust_hybrid + p_calibrated >= 0.80 had 52 forward entries, 32/19 W/L plus 1 flat, +$10.67, 20.5c avg/entry, 19.3% coverage, 62.7% win rate.
- Beta/temperature calibration on Brownian probability improved pure probability scores:
  - beta2_brownian forward Brier 0.16055, log loss 0.49944, rank tau about 1.0.
  - But its robust overlay was lower quality than ACI: about 87-88 forward entries, +$11.12 to +$11.32, 12.8c avg/entry, 53-54% win rate.
- RCPS-style classification-risk thresholds failed:
  - No usable alpha=0.20 threshold for any score.
  - Higher alpha thresholds did not pass forward loss/win/PnL gates.
  - This suggests classification loss is the wrong objective; a high loss rate can still be profitable because payoff sizes are asymmetric.
- E-process sanity check remains the promotion brake:
  - ACI p_cal >= 0.70 WFA-style overlay has forward max capital about 5.98 and all-window max about 11.50, below watch threshold 20 and promotion threshold 100.
  - ACI p_cal >= 0.80 has forward max capital about 5.44 and all-window max about 9.91.

What I need from the literature:
1. PnL-aware selective prediction:
   - conformal/risk-control methods for expected utility, not just classification error;
   - cost-sensitive selective classification;
   - reject-option classification with asymmetric payoff;
   - conformal decision theory or risk-controlling decisions with user-defined loss functions.
2. Threshold selection under nonstationary financial payoffs:
   - online threshold adaptation using prequential utility;
   - validation protocols for small rolling samples;
   - methods that prevent threshold overfitting when the chosen threshold is repeatedly 0.70 across WFA splits.
3. E-process design and sample sizing for small positive-edge strategies:
   - how to choose lambda without tuning on the same data;
   - expected detection time/power for edges around 10-20 cents per accepted trade;
   - e-processes for bounded but asymmetric PnL rather than binary wins/losses.
4. Calibration vs decision separation:
   - when a smoother beta/temperature calibrator should replace ACI for fair value;
   - when ACI should remain a risk overlay despite worse Brier/log loss;
   - how to combine a smooth calibrated probability with an online uncertainty/risk threshold.
